COMPANY OVERVIEW: GreenLeaf Organics

GreenLeaf Organics is a mid-sized organic food producer founded in 2015, 
headquartered in Portland, Oregon. The company specializes in packaged 
organic snacks, including granola bars, dried fruit, and trail mixes, 
distributed across 1,200 retail locations in the Pacific Northwest.

OPERATIONS:
GreenLeaf operates two production facilities — a 50,000 sq ft primary 
plant in Portland and a smaller co-packing facility in Salem. The company 
employs approximately 180 people across production, sales, and corporate 
functions.

SUPPLY CHAIN:
Raw materials are sourced from over 40 certified organic farms within a 
300-mile radius, supporting a "farm to shelf" sustainability narrative 
central to the brand's marketing.

GROWTH STRATEGY:
The company plans to expand into 3 additional states over the next 18 
months and is evaluating a direct-to-consumer e-commerce channel to 
complement retail distribution. Management has identified increasing 
ingredient costs and retail shelf-space competition as primary challenges 
to the expansion timeline.

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS:
GreenLeaf recently achieved B-Corp certification and launched a new 
product line of protein-enhanced snack bars, which account for 12% of 
total revenue within the first quarter of launch.
